Common questions about car accident cases

What does it cost to hire Sargon Law Group?

Nothing up front. The firm accepts cases on a contingency fee basis, meaning you do not pay until it recovers compensation for you. The consultation is free.

How long do I have to file a car accident claim in Arizona?

Generally two years from the accident date for a personal injury lawsuit. Some claims expire within 180 days, so the sooner you ask, the better.

What if I was partly at fault for the accident?

Partial fault does not automatically end a claim. Arizona weighs each driver's share of the blame, and the firm handles cases where fault is disputed or shared. Answer the screener honestly and the intake team will look at it.

Should I talk to the insurance company first?

The firm recommends consulting a car accident attorney before you give any insurance company a statement. Adjusters are trained to limit what the insurer pays.

I already have a lawyer. Can I still submit?

Yes. The screener asks whether you're working with a lawyer and whether you're thinking about switching. If you are, the intake team will talk it through with you.

Do you speak Spanish?
